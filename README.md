## Tue 23 Feb 2027 - Río Grey Kayak

| Time | Activity and transport |
| --- | --- |
| 07:30 | Breakfast. Set out a synthetic base layer, hat, gloves, sunscreen and a complete change of clothes. |
| 08:15-08:30 | Drive to Bigfoot Base Serrano, at the entrance to Villa Río Serrano on route Y-290. |
| 08:30-09:15 | Check in for the morning half-day. Fit the supplied wetsuit, jacket, boots, spray skirt and life jacket, then complete the safety briefing. |
| 09:15-13:00 | Guided kayak, about 3-4 hours including the operator's transfer. The group is taken to the Grey beach put-in, then paddles the Pingo River onto the Lago Grey peninsula and down Río Grey to the Grey bridge, and back. Drifted icebergs collect at the peninsula; on a clear day Grey Glacier is visible at the far end of the lake. Wind or water level can shorten or cancel the outing. |
| 13:15-13:30 | Drive back to the hotel. |
| 13:30-15:00 | Hot lunch. |
| 15:00-18:30 | Rest at the hotel. |
| 18:30 | Pack and arrange a breakfast box for tomorrow. |
| 19:30 | Dinner, then an early night. |

No previous kayaking is required. Book Bigfoot's morning half-day from this base, and confirm the check-in time, the duration and the minimum group. Carry the prepaid park ticket; the put-in is inside the park.

The glacier-wall kayak starts beside Refugio Grey. Reaching it for a same-day return means a Grey III crossing, and the other departures expect a night at the refugio. This half-day stays in Villa Río Serrano, so the night and the 24 February departure stay as planned. A full-day paddle is about six hours, and the 15:30 half-day ends too late to pack for a 06:00 start. If wind cancels the kayak, spend the afternoon at the hotel. The cancellation does not move the 24 February flights.
 
**Overnight:** Torres del Paine, same hotel (night 3 of 3).
